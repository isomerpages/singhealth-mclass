<h2>OUR<br>PURPOSE</h2>
<p>The purpose of the Singapore Community of Practice in Social Prescribing
(SCOMP)&nbsp; is to foster a dynamic and collaborative ecosystem where
diverse stakeholders, including healthcare professionals, community leaders,&nbsp;
researchers, and individuals come together to collectively advance the
understanding, implementation, and impact of social prescribing interventions.
The CoP serves as a platform for sharing knowledge, exchanging best practices,
and co-creating innovative strategies that enhance well-being, strengthen
community connections, and promote holistic health through the integration
of arts, culture, and community engagement.</p>
<p>A collaborative network of healthcare professionals, community leaders,
and practitioners committed to advancing social prescribing for health
and well-being in Singapore.</p>
